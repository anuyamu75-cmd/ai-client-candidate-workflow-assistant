# Sample Input

## Role

Customer Support Specialist

## Company Context

A growing software company is hiring a remote Customer Support Specialist to support customers using its SaaS platform.

## Location

Remote, outside the United States.

## Schedule

US business hours.

## Responsibilities

* Respond to customer questions by email and chat.
* Troubleshoot product-related issues.
* Guide customers through basic workflows.
* Document recurring issues.
* Escalate technical problems to the appropriate internal team.
* Maintain a positive customer experience.

## Requirements

* 1+ year of customer-facing experience.
* Strong written and verbal English.
* Comfortable learning SaaS products.
* Strong problem-solving skills.
* Remote-work capability.
* Technical support experience is preferred but not required.
