# AI Client & Candidate Workflow Assistant

A practical AI-assisted workflow for turning a hiring requirement into a structured sourcing, communication, and stakeholder-update plan.

## Why I built this

I work in a client-facing recruiting environment where a lot of time can be spent translating requirements, preparing outreach, creating screening questions, and keeping stakeholders updated.

I built this workflow to explore how AI can reduce repetitive work while keeping human judgment in the loop.

## What it does

Given a job requirement, the workflow helps produce:

* A concise requirement brief
* Must-have and nice-to-have criteria
* Candidate screening questions
* Personalized candidate outreach
* A follow-up sequence
* A client/stakeholder update
* Suggested next actions

## Workflow

Job requirement
↓
Requirement extraction
↓
Prioritized criteria
↓
Screening questions
↓
Candidate outreach
↓
Follow-up plan
↓
Client update
↓
Human review + action

## Human-in-the-loop principle

AI is used for structure, drafting, and repetitive preparation — not final hiring decisions.

A human should verify important information before communication or action.

## What I learned

Building this workflow helped me think about AI less as a replacement for relationship-driven work and more as a way to remove repetitive steps from a process.

The most useful approach was breaking the workflow into smaller stages, with each stage having a clear input and output.

## Future iteration

A future version could connect the workflow to a no-code automation platform so that a submitted requirement automatically creates the brief, screening guide, outreach draft, and stakeholder update.
