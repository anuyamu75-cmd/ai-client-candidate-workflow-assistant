# AI Workflow Quality Checklist

This checklist is used to review AI-generated workflow outputs before they are used externally.

## Accuracy

* [ ] Does every factual statement come from the original input?
* [ ] Did AI invent any company, candidate, skill, or requirement?
* [ ] Are assumptions clearly identified?
* [ ] Are missing details marked as "Not provided"?

## Communication

* [ ] Is the message concise and easy to understand?
* [ ] Does it sound natural and human?
* [ ] Is the call to action clear?
* [ ] Does it avoid exaggerated or unsupported claims?

## Customer and Candidate Experience

* [ ] Is the communication respectful?
* [ ] Does it address the recipient's likely needs?
* [ ] Does it avoid unnecessary pressure?
* [ ] Does it provide clear next steps?

## Human Judgment

* [ ] Have I reviewed the AI output?
* [ ] Have I checked important details?
* [ ] Have I verified information before sharing it?
* [ ] Am I making the final decision rather than delegating it to AI?

## Continuous Improvement

* [ ] What part of the workflow saved time?
* [ ] What output required the most editing?
* [ ] What information was frequently missing?
* [ ] What should be improved in the next version?
